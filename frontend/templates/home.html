How to test

  1. Open Visit page, enter Height/Weight/HbA1c (e.g., 170, 78, 9.4) → BMI fills, educator checkbox appears.
  2. Click Save Visit.
  3. Open Notifications or Tasks and click Process Events Now.
  4. Refresh the lists to see the new notification and a task due in 7 days.
